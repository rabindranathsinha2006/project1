Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

At THE ULTIMATE SHOPPING, we collect personal details necessary to process and fulfill your online orders. This includes your name, shipping address, mobile phone number, email address, and order transaction records.

2. Payment Security & Integrity

We respect customer financial security. We do not store raw credit card numbers or banking passwords. All online payments are handled securely through encrypted payment gateways.

3. Use of Cookies and Sessions

Our platform uses session cookies to remember items in your shopping cart, preserve your theme preference (Dark Mode or Light Mode), and maintain secure authenticated access to your account.

4. Disclosure to Logistics Partners

We only share required shipping information (recipient name, address, and contact number) with authorized courier partners solely for the purpose of package delivery and tracking.

5. Customer Rights & Data Protection

You have full authority to view, modify, or delete your saved shipping addresses, price drop alerts, and personal profile information at any time from your Account Dashboard.
